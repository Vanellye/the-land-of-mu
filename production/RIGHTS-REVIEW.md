# Rights Review — Mandatory Release Gate

**Current status: NOT REVIEWED — no public distribution authorized.**

This document defines a process; it does not certify ownership, originality, clearance, or permission for any asset.

## Asset-by-asset review

For every composition, lyric, sample, loop, soundfont, performance, artwork element, and third-party contribution, record:
- Asset ID and exact title/path
- Creator and rights holder
- Source URL, repository, or purchase/license record
- Rights basis and documented permission
- Scope: reproduction, adaptation, distribution, streaming, synchronization, territory, duration
- Restrictions and attribution requirements
- Evidence location (do not commit confidential contracts)
- Human reviewer, decision (pending / cleared / restricted / rejected), and date

## Review steps
1. Inventory every asset and provenance.
2. Identify all contributors and rights holders.
3. Verify rights basis and exact permitted uses.
4. Check sample, loop, soundfont, plugin preset, and artwork terms.
5. Resolve missing permissions.
6. Record reviewer, evidence reference, scope, and decision.
7. Block release if any material asset is pending, restricted beyond intended use, or rejected.

Musical sketches and motif descriptions are prompts, not proof of ownership of any third-party recording, composition, or sample. New lyrics and arrangements must be reviewed independently.

No asset is cleared by default. Complete one record per track and third-party asset before marking a release candidate rights-cleared.
