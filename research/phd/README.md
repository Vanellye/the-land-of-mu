# .PhD Research Workspace

Store research source files and notes related to composition, acoustics, psychoacoustics, signal processing, music information retrieval, synthesis, and audio evaluation.

For each research item, preserve:
- Original filename and source
- Author, publication, date, and citation
- Research question and method
- Data provenance and permissions
- Results, limitations, and uncertainty
- Reproducible code or analysis where applicable

Do not present an unreviewed hypothesis as a verified finding. Keep source documents unchanged; add annotations and derived work separately. Do not commit copyrighted full-text papers unless redistribution rights permit it.
